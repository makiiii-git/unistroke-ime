Uni-Stroke IME Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the Android app “Uni-Stroke IME” (the “App”) handles information about its users. It is a translation of the Japanese policy; if the two differ, the Japanese version prevails.

1. What the App does

The App is a keyboard (input method) for English and Japanese in which each character is written with a single stroke. Stroke recognition, word suggestions, and kana-kanji conversion are all processed on the device.

A keyboard handles every character its user types. The App does not send what you type to the developer. The Google Play version of the App does not access the network while you type.

2. Information handled and why

2.1 Information kept only on the device (never sent out)

  • Characters and strokes you write: recognized and converted on the device and passed to the app you are typing in. They are not sent to the developer’s servers or anywhere else.
  • Typing history (for suggestions): words and phrases you commit are stored only on the device (up to 500 entries for Japanese and 1,000 words for English) and used to show suggestions. You can erase them from Settings at any time. Nothing typed into password fields, or into fields where the app has refused learning (such as incognito tabs), is stored.
  • Learned handwriting: the shapes of your strokes are stored only on the device and used to improve recognition. You can erase them from Settings, one character at a time or all at once.
  • Voice input (optional): the microphone is used only while voice input is active, after you start it by holding a finger in the writing zone. By default only on-device speech recognition is used, and neither the audio nor the recognized text leaves the device. The App never records or saves audio. Voice input is disabled in password fields.
  • Settings and trial information: your settings, the date and time the trial started, and whether you have purchased Premium are stored only on the device.

If Android backup is turned on, this information may be included in the backup to your own Google account. That backup is handled by Google, and the developer cannot access its contents.

2.2 Information sent to third-party services

  • Speech recognition service (optional, off by default): only if you change the voice input setting to “Automatic”, and only when on-device recognition is unavailable, audio is passed to the speech recognition service installed on the device (such as Google’s). How that service handles audio is governed by its provider’s privacy policy.
  • In-app purchase (Google Play): purchasing Premium and checking whether it has been purchased are handled by Google Play. This check happens only when you open the App’s own screens, never while you type. The developer has no access to your payment information, such as credit card numbers. For details, see Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Downloading and checking for updates to the extended dictionary (GitHub): the App connects to GitHub when you choose to download the extended Japanese dictionary, and, if automatic update checks are on (off by default), when you open the App’s screens. Only a request for the dictionary file is sent, never anything you typed. GitHub processes connection information such as your IP address.
  • Features of the version distributed on GitHub only: the following two features are not in the Google Play version.
    • Online conversion (optional, off by default): only if you turn it on in Settings, just the hiragana reading you want converted is sent to Google’s conversion service. Nothing is sent from password, email, URL, or number fields, among others.
    • App update check: the App connects to the GitHub API to check for updates. The Google Play version is updated by Google Play.

3. Collection by the developer

The developer does not collect or store users’ personal information on any server of its own. The App shows no ads and does not send analytics or crash reports.

4. Sharing with third parties

The developer does not sell or provide users’ information to third parties (other than the processing by the services listed in 2.2).

5. Deleting your information

You can erase your typing history and learned handwriting from the App’s Settings. Uninstalling the App deletes all information it stored on the device.

6. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at any particular age group, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

Any changes to this policy will be announced on this page. Significant changes will also be noted in the App’s release notes or similar.

8. Contact

For questions about this policy, please open an issue in the GitHub repository.
